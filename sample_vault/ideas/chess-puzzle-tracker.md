# Chess puzzle tracker (idea)

A tiny daily habit: solve three puzzles, jot one sentence about what went
wrong, and let the streak do the motivating.

## Sketch

- One line per day: date, rating, and the themes that tripped me up.
- A weekly script — or a patient assistant — tallies the themes.
- Keep the whole thing under 100 lines so it never becomes homework.

## Questions to answer first

- Which puzzle themes hurt the most right now?
- Is blitz losing or is it just clock anxiety talking?
